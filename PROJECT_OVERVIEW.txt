                    KARTHIKEYAN ANALYSIS - SMART STUDENT PORTAL
         Comprehensive Academic, Examination & Digital Learning Platform
                                CLIENT OVERVIEW

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Karthikeyan Analysis Student Portal is an all-in-one, modern digital 
academy platform designed specifically for competitive exam coaching centers, 
educational institutes, and colleges. 

It seamlessly combines:
  * Official Computer-Based Testing (CBT) modeled on national exam standards
  * Interactive Live Online Classes with automatic attendance & recordings
  * Anti-Piracy Protected Study Materials (Video lessons & PDF notes)
  * End-to-End Online Admissions & Batch Enrollment workflows
  * Public Open-Candidate Mock Test Portals with instant Hall Tickets
  * Live Proctoring, Anti-Cheating Controls & Performance Analytics

The system automates the complete student lifecycle—from initial public 
registration and fee-linked batch enrollment to daily interactive learning, 
live testing, and deep academic analytics.


================================================================================
2. MAJOR PLATFORM MODULES & CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE A: COMPUTER-BASED TEST (CBT) EXAMINATION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A full-featured exam engine engineered to mirror high-stakes national competitive 
exam interfaces (such as NTA, CSIR, UPSC, or State PSC).

Key Features:
  • Realistic Exam Interface:
    - Official color-coded Question Palette (Answered, Not Answered, Marked 
      for Review, Not Visited).
    - Clear Response, Save & Next, and Mark for Review & Next actions.
    - Synchronized countdown timer with automatic test submission on expiry.
    - Full-screen distraction-free test environment.

  • Multi-Part / Multi-Subject Test Structuring:
    - Organize exams into labeled parts (e.g., Part A: Mathematics, Part B: 
      Statistics, Part C: Economics) with individual sectional marks.

  • Flexible Marking & Scoring:
    - Customizable positive marks per question (1, 1.5, 2 marks, etc.).
    - Configurable negative marking per wrong answer to discourage guessing.

  • Anti-Cheating & Exam Lockdown:
    - Question order randomization (shuffled for every candidate).
    - Right-click, text selection, copy-pasting, and screenshot keys disabled.
    - Browser auto-complete, spellcheck, and translation disabled during tests.
    - Single-session lock to prevent taking the test from multiple tabs/devices.

  • Immediate or Scheduled Results:
    - Admin chooses when students view solutions: immediately after completion, 
      only after the test window ends, or kept hidden for official release.

  • Comprehensive Digital Response Sheets:
    - Students and administrators can view complete question-by-question 
      response sheets with selected answers, correct answer keys, detailed 
      explanations, and instant PDF download.

Client Benefits:
  * Prepares students for real competitive exam environments, boosting their 
    confidence and scores.
  * Completely eliminates paper printing, physical hall arrangements, and 
    manual answer evaluation costs.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE B: LIVE TEST PROCTORING & IN-PROGRESS MONITORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gives exam controllers full visibility and authority during live test sessions.

Key Features:
  • Real-Time Invigilation Dashboard:
    - Live list of all students currently taking the test.
    - Visual indicators for time remaining (critical alerts for candidates with 
      under 5 minutes or 1 minute remaining).
    - Inactivity tracking (flags if a candidate has stopped interacting).

  • Remote Webcam Video Proctoring:
    - Faculty and invigilators can view live video feeds of students taking 
      their exams to verify student identity and prevent unauthorized assistance.

  • Active Proctor Interventions:
    - Broadcast live announcements or send direct warning messages to a 
      specific student's screen in real time.
    - Emergency Rejoin Approval: If a student accidentally gets disconnected 
      due to power/network loss, the admin can approve an emergency re-entry.
    - One-Click Force Submit: Admins can force-submit an exam if a student 
      violates exam integrity.

Client Benefits:
  * Eliminates cheating and proxy test-taking in remote online examinations.
  * Gives the institution total command and supervisory control over all tests.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE C: LIVE INTERACTIVE CLASSES & VIRTUAL CLASSROOMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A built-in video conferencing classroom tailored specifically for teaching, 
eliminating the need to distribute external third-party meeting links.

Key Features:
  • One-Click Class Entry:
    - Students join directly from their portal without copying links or codes.
    - Automatic restriction to assigned batches only.

  • Interactive Teaching Tools:
    - Crystal-clear audio, HD video, and ultra-smooth screen sharing.
    - Dedicated host and co-host controls (mute/unmute, participant management).

  • Automatic Attendance Tracking:
    - The portal automatically records when every student joins and leaves 
      the class, providing accurate attendance duration reports.

  • Built-In Cloud Recording & Library:
    - Classes can be recorded and archived directly into the student video 
      library, allowing students to rewatch missed lectures anytime.

Client Benefits:
  * Keeps all student traffic inside the institution's own branded portal.
  * No separate expensive Zoom or Google Meet enterprise subscriptions needed.
  * Solves the issue of missed lectures with automatic student access to archives.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE D: ANTI-PIRACY PROTECTED DIGITAL CONTENT LIBRARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Protect your institution's valuable intellectual property (study notes, ebooks, 
and proprietary recorded video lectures).

Key Features:
  • Dynamic Moving Watermark on PDF Notes:
    - When a student opens a study document, a floating, dynamic watermark 
      displaying the student's unique Name, Student ID, Email, and current 
      timestamp moves continuously across the screen.
    - Renders unauthorized screen recording, external mobile photography, 
      or sharing completely traceable to the leaking student.

  • Hardened Video Player:
    - Right-click, download options, and browser saving shortcuts are disabled.
    - Custom playback speeds (0.5x to 3x) for efficient student revision.
    - Watermarked with student credentials.

  • Granular Content Targeting:
    - Publish content to All Students, Specific Batches, or Selective 
      Individual Students based on enrolled courses.

Client Benefits:
  * 100% protection of proprietary study notes and lectures against illegal 
    forwarding, Telegram leaking, and commercial resale.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE E: STUDENT ENROLLMENT & BATCH MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Effortless handling of student rosters and multi-course programs.

Key Features:
  • Multi-Batch Enrollment & Quick Switching:
    - A student can belong to multiple batches simultaneously (e.g., Course 
      Batch + Test Series Batch) and easily switch between them with one click.

  • Single-Device Login Enforcement (Device Lockdown):
    - Each student account is locked to their primary device upon login.
    - Students cannot share their login credentials with friends or outsiders.
    - Admins can easily reset the registered device with one click if a student 
      legitimately upgrades their phone or laptop.

  • Dual Login Options:
    - Secure Google One-Tap Sign-In.
    - Admin-generated portal username/passcode fallback for students who 
      face device or Google account restrictions.

  • Bulk Student Management:
    - Bulk upload student rosters using Excel/CSV spreadsheets.
    - Search, filter by batch, activate, or deactivate student accounts instantly.

Client Benefits:
  * Eliminates unauthorized credential sharing, protecting institute tuition revenue.
  * Saves administrative staff hundreds of hours of manual roster maintenance.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE F: ONLINE ADMISSION FORMS & SCHEDULED ENROLLMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modernizes and digitizes the admission workflow.

Key Features:
  • Public Admission Portal:
    - Prospective students can submit applications online with personal details, 
      educational qualifications (UG/PG), subject preferences, and photo uploads.
    - Built-in legal declarations and agreement checkboxes.
    - Direct submission to admin queue for verification and enrollment.

  • Scheduled & Batch-Specific Enrollment Links:
    - Generate time-limited enrollment application links for specific batches.
    - Automated admission opening and closing schedules.
    - Download official completed student enrollment forms as professional 
      PDF applications.
    - One-click export of all admission responses directly to Excel.

Client Benefits:
  * Transitions the institution into a modern, 100% paperless admission office.
  * Captures leads and student applications round the clock.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE G: PUBLIC CBT PORTAL & INSTANT HALL TICKETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enables the institution to conduct open-access state-level or national mock 
scholarship tests to attract thousands of new candidates.

Key Features:
  • Open Registration & Candidate Login:
    - Public candidates can register independently with their contact details.
    - Candidate portal for accessing allotted open tests.

  • Instant Official Hall Ticket Generation:
    - Generates branded, official Hall Tickets complete with:
      * Candidate photo and personal details
      * Roll number / Registration number
      * Examination schedule and reporting timings
      * Subject breakdown and candidate instructions
      * One-click PDF download and printable A4 layout.

Client Benefits:
  * Serves as a high-conversion marketing and talent-hunting funnel to bring in 
    thousands of new paid enrollments.
  * Mirrors professional government/university examination systems.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE H: PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS, LEADERBOARDS & EXCEL REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Turn raw test data into actionable academic insights.

Key Features:
  • Batch-Wise & Test-Wise Analytics:
    - Track attendance percentage, average marks, minimum and maximum marks.
    - Sectional performance analysis across subjects.

  • Student Performance Matrix:
    - Longitudinal tracking of how individual students perform across 
      multiple consecutive tests.
    - Identify weak students who need extra academic intervention.

  • Exportable Excel Reports:
    - Full attendance matrices, candidate test scores, and detailed section 
      breakdowns can be downloaded as Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets anytime.

Client Benefits:
  * Enables evidence-based counseling and parent-teacher performance reviews.
  * Provides management with clear metrics on student improvement over time.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE I: CO-HOST & FACULTY ROLE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Secure team delegation without exposing sensitive student data.

Key Features:
  • Co-Host / Faculty Accounts:
    - Super-admin can create dedicated accounts for assistant teachers, 
      subject faculties, and invigilators.
    - Restricted permissions: Co-hosts can manage live classes and conduct 
      live tests, but cannot modify core portal settings, batch finances, or 
      student records.

Client Benefits:
  * Allows smooth delegation of teaching and invigilation duties without risk 
    to platform security or administrative data.
